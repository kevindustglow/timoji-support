A little guidance, at your pace.

Your first timer

How do I set a timer?

timoji opens on the timer. Tap 15, 30, or 60 under the ring to choose a timer. Tap a sticker in the tray below to place it in the ring, drag the ring’s end handle to set the time, then tap GO. timoji remembers the timer you used last. To see every sticker, tap All stickers at the end of the tray.

What is different about 15, 30, and 60?

15 is for one activity, from 1 to 15 minutes. 30 and 60 let you arrange several activities in one timer. All three are included in Free. These are time limits, so you do not need to fill the whole ring.

How do I arrange several activities?

In 30 or 60, tap stickers in the order you want to do them. Each new activity gets up to 10 minutes, depending on the space left, and needs at least 5 minutes. To change one, drag a sticker from the tray onto it; its time stays the same. To change the order, press and hold a sticker in the ring, then drag it onto another one to swap them, together with their times. Move the boundary between two activities to share time between them.

How do I change the time before starting?

Drag the ring’s end handle, or use −5 min and +5 min. In a multi-activity plan, move the boundary between two activities. Each planned activity in 30 or 60 needs at least 5 minutes, so the plan doesn’t feel rushed for a child. GO becomes available when an activity and a valid time are set.

How do I pause, resume, or add time?

Tap Pause while the timer is running. While paused, use + or − to change the current activity by 5 minutes, then tap Resume. Without an edit, Resume continues from the time you paused. While paused, − can bring the current activity down to 1 minute for a small last-minute change. Activities still to come keep at least 5 minutes until it is their turn. 15 stays within 1–15 minutes, and 30 or 60 cannot go past the end of the ring. To switch to another timer, end this one first.

Can we change the order while the timer is running?

Yes. Pause, then tap an upcoming sticker to do it first. The current activity moves to next, with its remaining time. You can also remove an upcoming activity. These changes affect this timer only.

What do Again, Next, and Done do?

When an activity ends and another follows, Again repeats the completed activity for its set duration; Next starts the next one. After the last activity, Done closes this timer. The +5 min or +10 min button continues the last activity: 5 extra minutes in 15 or 30, or 10 in 60.

Can the next activity start automatically?

With two or more activities, choose Pause or Auto-continue under After an activity. Pause leaves the choice to you. Auto-continue shows a short, quiet completion moment, then moves on after about 2.5 seconds. Changing this option while a completed activity is waiting applies to later transitions; use Again or Next for the one on screen.

Why does the screen look dimmer while running?

The softer screen keeps the sticker and time easy to notice. Close and Menu remain available. Opening Menu does not pause the clock; use Pause when you need a break. Tapping the background does not brighten the running screen. Turning the device sideways changes the layout and does not lock the controls. To change the theme, open Menu → Settings → Theme.

Will timoji ring when time is up?

Yes, if Time-up alert is on. When time is up, timoji rings like an alarm, even in Silent mode, and your iPhone or Apple Watch can vibrate. Before your first timer, timoji asks whether you want this; tap Allow, then allow alarms in the system prompt. Tap Stop on the alarm to silence it. You can turn it off any time in Menu → Settings. Sound Effects, in the same place, controls the softer sounds between activities.

Activity stickers

Where are all the stickers?

Tap All stickers at the end of the tray, or open Menu → Activity Stickers. Browse by category, Favorites, or Recent, then tap a sticker to place it in the timer. All built-in stickers are included in Free and cannot be deleted.

How do I keep favorites close?

In Activity Stickers, tap Edit, then tap the heart on a sticker. You can also press and hold a sticker and choose Favorite. Favorites come first in the tray. Tap Done when you finish.

How do I make my own sticker? · Plus

In Activity Stickers, tap New sticker. Choose a picture from Photos, take one with Camera, or use Create an image when Apple Image Playground is available. Pinch to zoom, move and rotate the picture inside the crop; Reset Crop lets you start over. Tap Next, enter an activity name of up to 20 characters, then Save. Your stickers appear first, in My stickers.

Can I use Image Playground or Genmoji?

On supported devices and systems, Create an image opens Apple’s image tools. The styles and Genmoji options you see are supplied by Apple. Confirm the result in the system sheet, then finish Preview → activity name → Save in timoji. The system checkmark alone does not save a named activity. Genmoji is for personal, non-commercial use.

How do I edit or remove my own sticker?

Press and hold your sticker, then choose Edit sticker to rename it or replace its photo, or Remove to delete it. To remove several, tap Edit and use the remove button on each sticker. Removing a sticker also removes its steps from saved routines; routines with other steps remain, and empty ones are archived. A timer already running keeps its current activity. Only your own stickers can be removed. Your sticker names stay as you wrote them when you change the app’s language.

Routines · Plus

Routines keep familiar activities ready for you and your child. Plan together, then adapt the routine when life changes.

What is a routine?

A routine saves the steps you repeat, ready to start again with one tap. For example, bedtime: brush teeth → bath → story. After school, you might plan homework → a board game → dinner, choosing the order together and leaving a little time for each. Different days can have different routines, such as “Monday after school” with piano practice. Start a routine when you are ready; it does not start automatically at a set time. Routines are included in Plus.

Routines were called Daily Flow in earlier versions. Your saved flows are still here.

How is a routine different from a 30- or 60-minute timer?

A 30- or 60-minute timer arranges activities for this time only. A routine is saved, so you do not have to arrange it again each day. Each step in a routine has its own time, so a routine can be longer than 60 minutes.

How do I create a routine?

Open Activity Stickers and choose Routines, or open Menu → Routines, then tap New routine. Tap Add activities and tap stickers in the order you want; tap a numbered sticker again to unselect it. A routine has 2–6 steps, and each step can be 5–30 minutes. Tap a step to adjust its time with + or −, or use its menu to change or remove the activity. Drag the handles to change the order. A name is optional; if you leave it empty, timoji uses the activity names. Then tap Save.

How do I start, edit, or delete a routine?

In Routines, tap Start on a routine to begin its first activity. Tap the routine to edit it, or use the ⋯ button for Edit and Delete; deleting asks for confirmation. Drag routines to change their order. Deleting a routine does not delete its stickers.

Can I change a routine while it is running?

Swipe the upcoming cards to see what comes later. While paused, tap the Next card to skip to the next activity; you can undo for a few seconds. You can also change the current activity’s time down to 1 minute, or tap an upcoming sticker to do it first. These changes do not rewrite the saved routine. To change it for next time, edit it in Routines.

Why can’t I start a routine?

A routine needs 2–6 activities to start. If removing a sticker leaves only one step, the routine is kept so you can edit it. Open it, add another activity, and Save.

Activity History · Plus

See the activities you’ve done together.

What does Activity History show?

Open Menu → Activity History. Choose Week or Month to see total time, the days you used timoji, and a daily chart. Tap a day for details, or choose an activity to see only that one. Each finished activity is recorded with its name, start and end times, and minutes.

Which activities are recorded?

Activities are recorded while you have Plus and Record activity time is on. Activities you finished before buying Plus are not recorded. To stop recording, turn off Record activity time in Manage History.

How do I remove, export, or clear records?

Swipe left on a record to remove it. In Manage History, you can export as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a timoji Backup, import a timoji Backup, or clear one activity’s history or everything. Export a copy first if you might want the records later.

What’s the difference between CSV and a timoji Backup?

Both include all your records. Choose by what you want to do with them.

Spreadsheet (CSV) is for reading or printing your records in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets. Each row is one activity, with the date, start and end times, focused minutes, and whether it was finished. timoji can’t import a CSV file.

timoji Backup is for keeping a copy or moving records to another device. Only timoji opens it, and it’s the only file you can import. Importing adds records that aren’t on this device yet. Records already here are skipped, and nothing is replaced.

Where are my records kept?

Records are saved on this device. If iCloud Backup is on, they’re included in it. timoji doesn’t collect them. Exports are saved where you choose, like Files, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive; timoji doesn’t sign in to any service. To move records to another device, export a timoji Backup and import it there. Purchases & troubleshooting

What is included in Free and Plus?

Free includes 15, 30, and 60-minute timers, all built-in stickers, several activities in one 30 or 60-minute timer, Favorites, Recent, themes, sound, Time-up alert, and Live Activity. Plus adds your own stickers, Routines, and Activity History. Plus is a one-time in-app purchase, with no subscription or separate timoji account. The app shows the price for your App Store region.

I bought Plus. How do I restore it?

Open Menu → My timoji (or My timoji Plus) and use Restore Purchases. Make sure Media & Purchases uses the Apple Account that bought Plus and that you have an internet connection. Let Apple finish checking the purchase, then return to the timer. Cancelling a purchase or a pending approval does not prevent you from using Free.

Plus or its price is not loading. What should I try?

Check your connection and App Store sign-in, then tap Try again in My timoji. If you already bought Plus, try Restore Purchases. If Apple shows a purchase as pending, wait for it to complete. Read the price in Apple’s confirmation before agreeing. If the purchase completed but Plus remains unavailable, contact us with the app version and the message you see; do not send payment details.

I cannot find Create an image.

Plus unlocks your own stickers, but Apple decides whether Image Playground is available. Check that your device and OS support Apple Intelligence, that it is enabled, and that the device language and Siri language are set to the same supported language. Availability also depends on Apple’s region and feature support. Photos and Camera remain alternatives. See Apple’s current requirements.

A picture will not load or Camera will not open.

Try selecting the picture again; an iCloud photo may need to download first. For Camera, check timoji’s camera permission in Settings and any device restrictions. You can cancel and use Photos instead. If an error remains, keep the original image and contact us with the steps and message—there is no need to send a child’s photo.

Why does my transparent picture have a solid background?

Versions before 1.5.1 could save an imported picture as JPEG, which cannot keep transparency. Newer versions keep transparency in PNG imports. Replace the sticker’s photo by importing the original transparent file again. Transparency already lost in an old JPEG cannot be restored automatically.

Why didn’t the alarm ring when time was up?

Check that Time-up alert is on in Menu → Settings. If it shows Alarms are off, allow alarms for timoji in the iPhone or iPad Settings app. The alarm rings even in Silent mode. Sound Effects follow the device’s volume and silent settings.

Why don’t I see the Live Activity?

Check that Live Activities are allowed for timoji in Settings on a supported system. The Live Activity appears while a timer is running or paused, and closes when you return to timoji after it ends. Lock Screen controls may ask you to unlock and open the app. If its display seems out of date, open timoji and check the timer there.

Are my stickers and routines synced or backed up?

Your stickers, routines, and Activity History are saved on this device. timoji does not provide its own account or cloud sync. Apple device backups are separate. You can export Activity History in Manage History. Restore Purchases restores Plus access, not your stickers or records, so avoid deleting the app as a first troubleshooting step.

How can I get more help?

Email kevin926@me.com with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, timoji version, and what happened. A short description of the steps is helpful. Please leave out children’s photos, passwords, and payment details.
