Your first timer

How do I set a timer?

Choose 15, 30, or—if you have Plus—60 minutes. Swipe through the Sticker Tray and tap an activity to select it. Set the time, then tap GO. To browse the full activity library, open Pick a sticker; it opens a full-screen view. You can start with something familiar, such as reading together or getting ready to go out.

What is different about 15, 30, and 60?

15 · One Thing is for one activity, from 1 to 15 minutes. 30 · A Few Things lets you arrange several activities within 30 minutes and is included in Free. 60 · Take Your Time offers the same kind of planning with room for up to 60 minutes and requires Plus. These are time limits, so you do not need to fill the whole ring.

How do I arrange several activities?

In 30 or 60, choose stickers in the order you want to do them. Each new activity gets up to 10 minutes, depending on the space left, and needs at least 5 minutes. Tap an occupied part of the ring to work with that activity. Replacing its sticker keeps its time; removing it keeps the other activities’ times. If less than 5 minutes remain, selecting another sticker replaces an activity rather than adding a very short step.

How do I change the time before starting?

Use the + and − controls to adjust by 5 minutes. You can also adjust the ring’s end handle; in a multi-activity plan, move the boundary between two activities to share time between them. Each planned activity in 30 or 60 needs at least 5 minutes. GO becomes available when an activity and a valid time are set.

How do I pause, resume, or add time?

Tap Pause while the timer is running. While paused, use + or − to change the current activity by 5 minutes, then tap Resume. Without an edit, Resume continues from the time you paused. A limit can disable a control: 15 stays within 1–15 minutes, and edited activities in 30 or 60 need at least 5 minutes. If an activity has naturally counted down below that, you can still resume it as it is.

What do Again, Next, and Done do?

When an activity ends and another follows, Again repeats the completed activity for its set duration; Next starts the next one. After the last activity, Done closes this timer. The +5 min or +10 min button continues the last activity: 5 extra minutes in 15 or 30, or 10 in 60.

Can the next activity start automatically?

With two or more activities, choose Pause or Auto-continue under After an activity. Pause leaves the choice to you. Auto-continue shows a short, quiet completion moment, then moves on after about 2.5 seconds. Changing this option while a completed activity is waiting applies to later transitions; use Again or Next for the one on screen.

Why does the screen look dimmer while running?

The softer screen keeps the sticker and time easy to notice. Close, Theme, and Menu remain available. Opening Menu does not pause the clock; use Pause when you need a break. Tapping the background does not brighten the running screen. Turning the device sideways changes the layout and does not lock the controls.

My Activities & your own stickers

What can I do in My Activities for free?

All built-in stickers are included in Free. Open My Activities from the timer or Menu to browse them, add hearts to Favorites, and arrange the built-in group using the handles in list view. Recent fills as you use activities. Favorites and Recent have their own sorting, so they do not have drag handles. Built-in activities cannot be deleted.

How do I add a custom activity? · Plus

Open Add Activity, or open My Activities and tap the add button. Choose a picture from Photos, take one with Camera, or use Create an image when Apple Image Playground is available. After choosing or creating the picture, return to Preview. Pinch to zoom, move and rotate the picture inside the crop; Reset Crop lets you start over. Tap Next, enter an activity name of up to 20 characters, then Save. Saving adds it to your library; choose its sticker to use it in a timer.

Can I use Image Playground or Genmoji?

On supported devices and systems, Create an image opens Apple’s image tools. The styles and Genmoji options you see are supplied by Apple. Confirm the result in the system sheet, then finish Preview → activity name → Save in timoji. The system checkmark alone does not save a named activity. Genmoji is for personal, non-commercial use.

How do I edit a custom activity?

In My Activities, tap the activity to open its details. You can rename it or choose Replace photo to go through the picture and crop steps again. Use the list handles to reorder your custom group. Your own activity names stay as you wrote them when you change the app’s language.

How do I delete one or several custom activities?

Use the trash button for one activity. For several, tap Select activities in My Activities, select the ones you want, then Remove and read the confirmation. Cancel keeps your activities. Deleting an activity also removes its matching steps from saved Daily Flows. Flows with other steps remain; empty ones are archived. A timer already running keeps its current activity snapshot.

Daily Flow · Plus

What is Daily Flow?

Daily Flow lets you save activity sequences that fit your family’s everyday life, ready to start again with one tap. After school, you might plan bath time → homework → a board game → dinner, choosing the order together and leaving a little time for each activity. Different days can have different flows: Monday might include piano practice, while Tuesday has time for play dough. Save them as “Monday after school” and “Tuesday after school,” then choose the one that fits the day. You can adjust them as family life changes. Daily Flow is included in Plus.

How is Daily Flow different from a 30- or 60-minute plan?

A time plan arranges activities for this timer. Daily Flow saves a familiar sequence so you can use it again. An after-school flow with bath time, homework, a board game, and dinner saves you arranging it from scratch each day. You can also save separate flows for days with piano practice or play dough. Start the flow when you are ready; it does not start automatically on a particular weekday or at a set time.

How do I create and save a Daily Flow?

Open Daily Flow from the timer or Menu and tap Add Daily Flow. Choose activities, give the flow a name, and arrange 2–6 steps. Tap a step to expand it: adjust its time with + or −, choose Change activity, or remove it with the trash button. Each step can be 5–30 minutes in 5-minute steps. Drag the handles to set the order, choose Pause or Auto-continue, then Save Daily Flow.

How do I start, edit, or delete a saved flow?

In the Daily Flow list, tap the Play button beside a flow to start its first activity. Tap the flow itself to edit it, and save your changes. Press and hold the flow to open a menu with Edit and Remove; removal asks for confirmation. List handles change the order of your saved flows. Removing a saved flow does not delete its activity stickers.

Can I change a flow while it is running?

Pause the timer, then tap Change flow to adjust this run: skip, remove, reorder, or bring a later activity forward. These changes do not rewrite the saved Daily Flow. After an edit, use GO or Resume to continue. To change the routine for next time, edit it in the Daily Flow list.

Why is a flow’s Play button unavailable?

A flow needs 2–6 activities to start. If deleting a custom activity leaves only one step, the flow is kept so you can edit it. Open it, add another activity, and Save.

Purchases & troubleshooting

What is included in Free and Plus?

Free includes 15 and 30 minutes, all built-in activities, multi-activity planning in 30, Favorites, Recent, built-in activity management, themes, sound, and Live Activity. Plus adds 60 minutes, custom activities, and Daily Flow. Plus is a one-time in-app purchase, with no subscription or separate timoji account. The app shows the price for your App Store region.

I bought Plus. How do I restore it?

Open Menu → My timoji (or My timoji Plus) and use Restore Purchases. Make sure Media & Purchases uses the Apple Account that bought Plus and that you have an internet connection. Let Apple finish checking the purchase, then return to the timer. Cancelling a purchase or a pending approval does not prevent you from using Free.

Plus or its price is not loading. What should I try?

Check your connection and App Store sign-in, reopen timoji, and try Restore Purchases if you already bought Plus. If Apple shows a purchase as pending, wait for it to complete. Read the price in Apple’s confirmation before agreeing. If the purchase completed but Plus remains unavailable, contact us with the app version and the message you see; do not send payment details.

I cannot find Create an image.

Plus unlocks custom activities, but Apple decides whether Image Playground is available. Check that your device and OS support Apple Intelligence, that it is enabled, and that the device language and Siri language are set to the same supported language. Availability also depends on Apple’s region and feature support. Photos and Camera remain alternatives. See Apple’s current requirements.

A picture will not load or Camera will not open.

Try selecting the picture again; an iCloud photo may need to download first. For Camera, check timoji’s camera permission in Settings and any device restrictions. You can cancel and use Photos instead. If an error remains, keep the original image and contact us with the steps and message—there is no need to send a child’s photo.

Why does my transparent picture have a solid background?

An older import may have saved the picture as JPEG, which cannot retain transparency. A fix that preserves transparency in PNG imports is included in version 1.5.1, currently awaiting App Store review as of October 9, 2026. Once that update is available, replace the activity’s picture by importing the original transparent file again. Transparency already lost in an old JPEG cannot be restored automatically.

Why is there no sound or Live Activity?

Sound follows the device’s volume and silent settings; also check Sound in timoji’s Menu. For Live Activity, check that Live Activities are allowed for timoji in Settings on a supported system. Lock Screen controls may ask you to unlock and open the app. If its display seems out of date, open timoji and check the timer there.

Are my activities synced or backed up?

Custom activities and Daily Flows are saved on this device. timoji does not provide its own account or cloud sync. Apple device backups are separate. Restore Purchases restores Plus access, not your activity library, so avoid deleting the app as a first troubleshooting step.

How can I get more help?

Email kevin926@me.com with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, timoji version, and what happened. A short description of the steps is helpful. Please leave out children’s photos, passwords, and payment details.
