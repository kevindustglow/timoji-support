Overview

timoji is a visual timer that parents and children can use together to see time and plan everyday activities, operated by Chiang Chien Chu (Studio Kí.) in Taiwan. This policy covers timoji 1.7.0 and this Privacy and Support website.

timoji works without an app account. Activity and timer information is stored on your device rather than uploaded to a server operated by us. If you choose to email us, we use the information you send to respond to your request.

Information We Collect

timoji processes activity names, chosen photos and crops, favorites, activity order, saved routines, timer progress, and settings on your device. Recent activity times and completion records help resume timers and prevent repeated reminders. Local random IDs identify activities and sessions; they are not advertising or cross-app identifiers.

The system photo picker provides only the images you choose. Camera permission is requested when you choose Take a Photo. Imported images are resized and re-encoded for activity stickers. Photos and camera access are optional; built-in stickers work without them. timoji does not request location, contacts, microphone access, or full photo library access.

On supported devices and systems, you may use Apple’s Image Playground, including Genmoji, to create an activity image. Apple manages generation under its privacy terms; processing may use on-device models, Apple services, or extensions you choose in the system interface. timoji receives the image you accept and stores it locally. It does not send your library to a developer-operated image-generation service. This feature is optional; photos and built-in stickers remain available. See Apple Intelligence and Privacy.

This version has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. We do not sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or create advertising profiles. Information voluntarily sent by email and website hosting are described below.

How timoji Stores Data

Your activities, photos, routines, and timer settings are stored in local app storage. We do not operate a server that receives this library, and timoji has no separate cloud account or app-to-app sync service. Cropped sticker copies are also stored in a shared on-device App Group container so the Live Activity extension can display them. The Home Screen widgets read a small snapshot from the same container (sticker and routine names and the current timer state), kept on this device only.

With timoji Plus, Activity History records finished activities (activity name, start and end times, and focused minutes) on this device. If iCloud Backup is on, they’re included in it. timoji doesn’t collect them. In Manage History, you can export these records as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a timoji backup file, import a backup, clear the records, or turn recording off. Exports are saved only where you choose.

Device or iCloud backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings. These system backups are separate from timoji’s local storage. We cannot access, recover, or delete your local library or Apple backups. You can manage backups through Apple’s device and account settings.

Purchases

Apple handles timoji Plus payments and restores through the App Store. timoji checks verified StoreKit transactions on your device to unlock purchased features.

We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password. Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Children’s Privacy

Parents and children can use timoji together to see and understand time. The Free timer does not require an account, name, photo, age, or online profile. Parents or caregivers should manage purchases, custom photos, and external links.

timoji does not provide a parental-consent verification system. Please do not send children’s photos or unnecessary personal information by email. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, a parent or caregiver can contact us to request its deletion, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Third-Party Services

timoji uses Apple’s system services for purchases, optional photo selection and camera access, and optional local notifications. If you turn on Time-up alert, timoji also schedules alarms on your device through Apple’s AlarmKit. It does not register for developer-operated remote push notifications. Your device may share diagnostics with Apple according to your system settings.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub’s hosting information and Privacy Statement. The site itself adds no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, forms, or external fonts.

External links open services governed by their own policies. Following a link may disclose connection information, such as your IP address, to that service. Email handling is described in the next section.

Support Emails

If you email heytimoji@gmail.com, Chiang Chien Chu receives and replies through Gmail (Google). We use your email address, message, and any details you choose to provide to answer questions, troubleshoot issues, and respond to feedback or privacy requests.

Support emails are not forwarded to a CRM, helpdesk platform, analytics service, or another third-party service beyond Gmail (Google). We do not use them for marketing or user analysis.

Sending a message is optional and does not affect your use of timoji. Without relevant details, we may be unable to answer your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Data Retention

Local app information remains until you change or remove it, or delete the app and its data. Removing an activity also removes its matching steps from saved routines; a running session retains its activity copy until you close that session. Apple backups may retain earlier copies according to your settings.

We normally retain closed support emails for one year after the request is resolved, then delete them. Longer retention may be necessary for legal obligations, payment disputes, security incidents, or other necessary reasons. Gmail (Google) may retain deleted messages or backups according to its service behavior and settings. GitHub handles hosting logs under its own policies.

Your Rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, a copy of your information, restriction of processing, or to object to processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may have the right to withdraw it. You may also contact the relevant privacy authority.

For information you have emailed us, send your request to heytimoji@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify your request and respond appropriately. We cannot access information kept only on your device; use the app’s controls or delete the app and its data to remove it. Requests about data held independently by Apple, Google, or GitHub should be directed to those providers.

International Users

timoji is operated from Taiwan. If you contact us from another country, your message is handled by us in Taiwan through Gmail (Google). Apple, Google, and GitHub may process service information in countries outside your own under their respective policies.

Privacy rights and requirements vary by location. Contact us about your information or this policy, and we will consider your request under applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

If timoji’s data practices or this website change, we will update this policy and its “Last updated” date. Any additional notice required by applicable law will be provided.
